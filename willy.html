Who is Willy Wonka?
Willy Wonka is a fictional character created by Roald Dahl, who is the eccentric owner of the Wonka Chocolate Factory, first introduced in the 1964 novel Charlie and the Chocolate Factory. He is known as a famous inventor and chocolatier who seeks a worthy heir to take over his factory, a plot that is central to the book and its various adaptations.
What is Golden Ticket?
Golden Tickets are five special passes hidden in Wonka candy bars, which grant the finder and one family member a private tour of Willy Wonka's mysterious factory.
